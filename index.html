    01About

    About HackTrent

    HackTrent is Trent University's annual student hackathon. Over one weekend, students of every skill level team up to design, build, and demo projects, with workshops, mentors, free food, and prizes along the way.

    This year's theme is building for everyone. Projects are judged on creativity and technical skill, and on how usable they are for people with disabilities. No experience required, just curiosity.

    • Beginner-friendly workshops all weekend
    • Quiet room, prayer room, and gender-neutral washrooms
    • American Sign Language (ASL) interpretation for opening and closing ceremonies
    • Step-free access to every event space

    Accessibility statement

    This site aims to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA. You can use it with a keyboard alone or a screen reader. It follows your system's dark mode, reduced motion, and high contrast settings, and you can change the text size or theme from the header.

    Keyboard and form help
    • Tab and Shift+Tab move between links and controls.
    • Enter or Space activates buttons; Enter follows links.
    • Escape closes the find results.
    • Arrow keys choose an experience level once a radio button has focus.
    • If the form has mistakes, a summary appears with a link to each field.

    Found a barrier? Email access@hacktrent.ca and we'll reply within two business days.

    Privacy policy

    We collect only what you type into the application form. We use it to confirm your place, send schedule updates, and arrange any accessibility needs you tell us about. We delete it within 90 days of the event. We never sell or share it.

    Code of conduct and terms of use

    HackTrent is a harassment-free event for everyone, regardless of disability, gender, race, religion, or experience level. Be kind, be inclusive, and respect other people's space and equipment. Organizers may remove anyone who breaks these rules. Report concerns to any organizer or email hello@hacktrent.ca.
