    01 · About

    Welcome to HackTrent

    HackTrent is Trent University's annual student hackathon. Over one weekend, students of every skill level team up to design, build, and demo projects, with workshops, mentors, free food, and prizes along the way.

    This year's theme is building for everyone. Projects are judged on creativity and technical skill, and on how usable they are for people with disabilities. No experience required, just curiosity.

    • Beginner-friendly workshops all weekend
    • Quiet room, prayer room, and gender-neutral washrooms
    • ASL interpretation for opening and closing ceremonies
    • Step-free access to every event space
