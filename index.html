Welcome to HackTrent

HackTrent is Trent University's annual student hackathon. Over one weekend, students of every skill level team up to design, build, and demo projects — with workshops, mentors, free food, and prizes along the way.

This year's theme is building for everyone: projects are judged not only on creativity and technical skill, but on how usable they are for people with disabilities. No experience required — just curiosity.

Applications are open until November 30, 2026. Registration is free for all students.
